## The pitch

> ProcrastiBot: an AI that watches you procrastinate in real time, then emails your mother a weekly report grading precisely how much nothing you accomplished.

One sentence, 157 characters. An AI agent as the subject, deadpan founder confidence, a product name, and a problem nobody has — procrastination, reported to your mother.
